Extension privacy

Last updated: 7 November 2026 · Applies to Download with Majra 1.0.0

Download with Majra is a small Chromium extension whose only job is to hand a download to the Majra desktop app. This page explains what it can read, why it needs each permission, and where the data goes. It is written to be read, not to be skipped.

The short version

No analytics, no ads, no account, no data sold. The extension talks to the Majra app on your own PC. Asraab operates no server that receives your browsing or download data.

What the extension can access

The extension declares only the permissions it uses to capture a download and pass it to Majra. Chromium shows these permissions when you load or install it.

  • downloads — to notice a download you started, read its URL and filename, and route it to Majra instead of the browser.
  • cookies — to include the cookies of the page you are already signed in to, so the desktop app can fetch the same file you could fetch in the browser.
  • tabs — to know which page a download came from and to keep the hand-off tied to that tab.
  • contextMenus — to add the “Download with Majra” item to the link menu.
  • storage — to remember your extension settings, such as whether capture is on.
  • alarms — to retry a hand-off when the Majra app was not running yet.
  • <all_urls> — because a download can come from any site. The extension does not read page content to profile you.

Where the data goes

The URL, filename and cookies of the download you chose are sent to the Majra app running on the same computer, over a local connection. Settings stay in the browser's extension storage. Nothing is sent to Asraab, to an analytics service, or to any third-party server.

Majra then downloads the file directly from the original server, exactly as your browser would. That server sees the request, its cookies and your IP address, under its own privacy policy.

What the extension never does

  • It does not collect analytics, crash reports, or usage statistics.
  • It does not inject ads, change search results, or modify pages.
  • It does not sell, rent or share data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • It does not create an account, a profile, or a device fingerprint.
  • It does not download anything on its own. It only hands off a download you started.

Your control

You can turn capture off in the extension popup, remove the extension from chrome://extensions, and clear its storage at any time. Uninstalling the extension removes its local settings. Majra's own queue and history live on your PC and can be cleared from the app.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it, and the diff is visible in the public repository. Questions or a privacy concern can be opened as an issue on the Majra repository.

Plain summary

The extension exists to move one download from the browser to your own desktop app. That is the whole product. If it ever needs to do more, this page will say so first.
